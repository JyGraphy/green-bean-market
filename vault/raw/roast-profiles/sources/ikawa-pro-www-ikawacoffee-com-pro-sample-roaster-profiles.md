Featured Profiles
Roaster Kat – Cupping Profile
Kat Melheim is a roaster, barista, writer, artist, and all-around coffee person. She has been roasting for 7 years, but has been involved in the coffee industry for more than 10.
Kat has created this profile on her IKAWA Pro100x and uses it regularly for all the coffees she wants to cup and assess for quality.
This profile is suited for most origins and processing methods, which makes it a great starting point in your sample roasting.

Nomad Coffee, Spain – sample roasting profile
Nomad Coffee is an award-winning roastery and coffee shop based in Barcelona, Spain, started by Jordi Mestre from his love for coffee.
Nomad has been utilizing their IKAWA throughout these years of creating a coffee community around specialty coffee in Spain.
We can only be proud to be part of their story.

A Matter of Concrete – Baseline sample roasting profile by Rob Clarijs
Rob Clarijs, owner of A.M.O.C. in Rotterdam, Netherlands, and Coffee Masters Champion roasts every sample he gets in the roastery on his 6-year-old IKAWA using this profile. He then cups everything and adds his favourites to the iconic A.M.O.C. catalogue.
This simple profile will allow you to assess your green quickly and accurately.
