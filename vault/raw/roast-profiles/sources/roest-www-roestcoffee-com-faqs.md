+ Where is it made?
ROEST Sample Roasters are designed, engineered and manufactured in Norway, with the highest levels of quality control.
+ Where are the parts manufactured?
Our parts are mainly produced in Europe, India, and China.
Over time, we’ve been centralizing more of our production in Europe as we find reliable, high-quality manufacturers there. Production in India (last order placed earlier this year) and China is gradually being reduced as suitable European alternatives become available.
+ Do you ship worldwide?
We ship worldwide from Norway, excluding Cuba, Iran, Belarus, Russia, and Ukrania. Shipping cost is 100 EUR for Europe and 200 EUR outside of Europe.
